Welcome to our practical TrollStore guide. Built by developer @opa334, it changed app loading on iOS. Here, we share useful tips and safety advice to help you use your device well.

Installation & Jailbreak

  • Yes. It uses a core bug to run apps forever with no jailbreak needed.

  • No. It is not a jailbreak. It runs on stock iOS safely.

  • You can get it via Safari on supported devices, or use a PC tool.

Compatibility & What Is

  • It is an app installer. It lets you keep apps forever with no 7-day limit.

  • It uses a CoreTrust bug in iOS to trust third-party apps.

  • Key perks are zero limits, no weekly sync, push alerts, and full access.

  • It works on iOS 14.0 up to iOS 17.0. It fails on iOS 17.1 or newer.

  • AltStore needs weekly PC sync. TrollStore has zero expiration.

Troubleshooting, Safety & Management

  • Yes, it is safe and open source. Just download safe apps.

  • Get IPA files from safe GitHub pages or trusted community hubs.

  • If it crashes, refresh your helper in app settings right now.

  • Clear bad profiles in settings, reboot your phone, and try again.

  • Open app settings, tap uninstall, and remove the profile.
