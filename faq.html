Compatibility & What Is

TrollStore is an app installer for iOS. It lets you keep apps forever. They will never expire or crash after 7 days.

TrollStore bypasses standard app signing. It exploits a CoreTrust vulnerability in iOS. This trick lets the system treat third-party apps as trusted official software.

Key benefits include permanent app signing with zero 7-day expiration, unlimited app slots, no weekly computer sync, full push notifications, and root privilege access.

It supports iOS 14.0 up to iOS 17.0. It does not work on iOS 17.1, iOS 17.5, iOS 18, or any newer updates.

AltStore requires you to refresh apps every 7 days using a computer. TrollStore gives you permanent app signing with zero expiration and no weekly PC sync.

Troubleshooting, Safety & Management

Yes, TrollStore is safe and open-source. But downloading bad apps from unknown sites can risk your privacy. Always get apps from trusted places.

Always download IPA files from official developer GitHub repositories or verified community sites. Avoid random links to protect your privacy and security.

If it crashes or won't open, your helper app may not be linked correctly. Open TrollStore settings and refresh your persistence helper right away.

If you get an error code during installation, clear stuck profiles in Settings > General > VPN & Device Management, reboot your phone, and try again.

You can easily remove TrollStore. Go to the TrollStore app settings, select the uninstall option, and delete the associated persistence helper profile.
