Installation & Jailbreak

Yes. TrollStore uses a CoreTrust bug[cite: 4]. It lets you install apps forever without a jailbreak. It does not harm your phone or affect Apple Pay.

No. TrollStore is not a jailbreak. It is a tool that runs on normal iOS[cite: 4]. You do not need a jailbreak to use it. Your phone stays secure.

It depends on your iOS version. iOS 15.0 to 16.6.1 and some early iOS 17 versions work. Apple patched the bug in iOS 17.1 and iOS 18[cite: 4]. So, it does not work natively on newer versions.

On supported versions, you can install it right from Safari without a computer. If no web link works for your device, you must use a PC tool like Sideloadly.

Compatibility & What Is

TrollStore is an app installer for iOS[cite: 4]. It lets you keep apps forever. They will never expire or crash after 7 days.

Yes. TrollStore works great on modern iOS versions. It uses a core trust bug to install apps safely and cleanly.

It supports iOS 14.0 up to iOS 17.0. It does not work on iOS 17.1, iOS 17.5, iOS 18, or any newer updates.

Troubleshooting & Safety

Yes, TrollStore is safe and open-source. But downloading bad apps from unknown sites can risk your privacy. Always get apps from trusted places.

If it crashes or won't open, your helper app may not be linked correctly. Open TrollStore settings and refresh your persistence helper right away.
