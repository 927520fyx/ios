Compatibility & What Is TrollStore

  • TrollStore is an advanced permanent app installer. It lets you keep apps forever with no 7-day expiration limit.

  • TrollStore works by utilizing a CoreTrust bug in iOS to permanently trust third-party applications.

  • Key perks of TrollStore are zero certificate limits, no weekly PC sync, working push notifications, and full root access.

  • TrollStore works on iOS 14.0 up to iOS 17.0. It fails and is incompatible on iOS 17.1 or newer versions.

  • AltStore needs weekly PC sync and has active certificate limits. TrollStore has zero expiration and permanent signing.
